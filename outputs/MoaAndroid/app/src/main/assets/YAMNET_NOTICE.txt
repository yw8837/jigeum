YAMNet predicts broad AudioSet sound event categories. The app uses only
baby cry, laughter, and speech-like candidates as optional clues; these
scores do not measure an infant's intention, emotion, hunger, sleepiness,
health, or developmental status. Infant-specific performance has not been
validated by this app. Audio is processed on-device in RAM and not retained.
