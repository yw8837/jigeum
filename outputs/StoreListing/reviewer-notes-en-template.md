## English instructions for the configured build

No app account, sign-up, payment, or subscription trial is required for review. The reusable review credential is supplied in the separate Play Console access credential field. It works independently of the reviewer's location and can be reused on another installation. Internet access is required for activation and renewal. Review access grants the same camera, saved-video and PDF features as a valid full access pass; no purchase is made.

1. Launch **지금 내 기분이 그래**. Open **돌봄** (Care), then **구매 복원 · 이용권 관리** (Restore/manage access) to open the full access screen. Alternatively, tap **카메라로 살펴보기** (Observe with camera) on the home screen.
2. Expand **Play 심사 접근** (Play review access).
3. Paste the provided credential into **심사 접근 코드** (Review access code), then tap **심사 접근 확인** (Confirm review access).
4. After successful verification, tap the offered continuation to camera or video. Camera/microphone permission is requested only when the corresponding feature is used. Use your own non-sensitive test media. Saved videos are limited to 30 seconds/50 MB.
5. For free expression/record flows, return to **오늘**, tap **소리로 번역하기**, then **녹음 없이 직접 번역**. Select sound/context, choose or edit a phrase, then **이 문장 저장하기**. Existing records can be viewed and shared without a pass.
6. For PDF, open **아기말북** (Baby wordbook), select a month containing saved sample records, then tap **이 달의 아기말북 PDF 만들기** (Create this month's PDF). The PDF is generated on-device and offered through Android's system share sheet. Choose a compatible PDF viewer or saving/sharing application.
7. The app encrypts the reusable review credential on the device. Server-signed grants last up to 24 hours; reopening the app renews access online. After reboot, reconnect to the internet for confirmation. To remove review access, return to the access screen and tap **심사 접근 해제** (Disable review access).

Baby audio, images, videos and records stay on-device. The review request sends only package name, random installation identifier and the access credential. The result phrases are caregiver-confirmed expression examples, not medical/developmental diagnosis or decoded infant intent.
